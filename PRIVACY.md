**Privacy & Data Handling**

This tool processes everything in your browser.

1. The invoice PDF, any profile file and templates, and the filled form are never uploaded anywhere.
2. The page loads two open-source libraries from a public CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com): pdf.js, to read the PDF, and JSZip, to build the .docx. These are script downloads, not data uploads.
3. The browser saves your Details, the "Submitted by" name and a loaded profile in its local storage on your device only, so you do not have to re-enter them each time. "Remove profile", or clearing your browser's site data, removes them.
