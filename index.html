CRO Invoice Automator

A Marvelous Maura Model tool • by Maura Lavelle

Reads a CRO invoice or cost proposal (PDF) and turns it into a filled Word form, so one invoice gives both finance/accounting and science/operations what they need. Works with any CRO's invoice; SGS, TKL, IIVS and Hilltop invoices are read in more detail. Check every field before you download. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
